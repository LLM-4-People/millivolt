# Support

millivolt is pre-1.0 and maintained on a best-effort basis. There is no
response-time SLA.

- [Documentation index](docs/README.md)
- [Quickstart](README.md#quickstart)

- [Reverse proxy](docs/reverse-proxy.md)

## Where to ask

- **Usage, configuration, or deployment question:** open a
  [usage question](https://github.com/LLM-4-People/millivolt/issues/new?template=question.yml).
- **Reproducible defect:** open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/LLM-4-People/millivolt/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Concrete proposal:** open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/LLM-4-People/millivolt/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Security vulnerability:** use the
  [private advisory channel](https://github.com/LLM-4-People/millivolt/security/advisories/new).
  Do not open a public issue.

Search existing issues before opening a new one. Include the millivolt version
or revision and a sanitized configuration shape. Remove credentials, private
prompts, databases, and host-specific paths.
